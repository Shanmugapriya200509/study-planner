My Planner AI

An AI-powered personalized study planner for students

My Planner AI is an AI study planner that turns your syllabus topics and your deadline into a personalized, adaptive study plan. When your quiz results show a weak topic, the plan is rebuilt so that topic gets more revision time.

What you can do

  • Enter your subjects, units and topics, and set your target date and daily study hours
  • Get a daily and weekly study plan with study, revision and test sessions
  • Read AI-generated explanations of each topic at beginner, intermediate or advanced level
  • Take AI-generated multiple-choice quizzes and see your weak and strong topics
  • Adapt your remaining plan after quizzes or missed sessions
  • Track progress on a dashboard with charts
